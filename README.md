# SHOGI_AI

## What problems does it solve?

**SHOGI_AI** allows you to:
- Develop a complex project **on your own**
- Implement a game of a certain **complexity** (to be determined)
- Attempt to solve the game by developing **AI models**
- And optionally develop a user-friendly **graphical interface**

## Who is the typical user?

Depending on the AI chosen (I plan to implement 2 AIs), users could be either **people new to SHOGI** (a limited AI potentially followed by a tutorial) or **experienced players** looking for a challenge (I will apply all my knowledge to make the AI as comprehensive as possible, playing in under 100 ms).

## What are the competing applications?

I will base this solely on the concept of Shogi, a game I know quite well.

## How do I differentiate myself?

The unique feature of this project will be the most limited use possible during the development and concrete design phases; the use of the AI will be rigorously documented throughout the project.
